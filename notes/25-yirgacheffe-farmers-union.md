# Yirgacheffe Farmers Cooperative Union (YFCU)

**Structure:** 28 primary cooperatives, 300,000+ farmer members. Governed by general assembly + elected board.

**Key contact:** **Alemayehu Bekele** — Export Manager (12yrs). Fluent English, SCA certified. Visits Portland annually (SCA Expo).

**Our partnership:** Since 2021. 3-year rolling contract (renewed 2024). Volume: ~1,500 bags/year across Yirgacheffe, Kochere, Gedeb woredas.

**Traceability:** Lot-level to washing station (e.g., "Kochere Banko 74158"). Farm-gate price transparency: published annually.

**Community projects (funded by [[Farm Premium Fund]]):**
- 2023: Kochere washing station eco-pulper ($18K)
- 2024: Gedeb raised drying beds + canopy ($22K)
- 2025: Yirgacheffe cupping lab + Q-Grader training ($28K)

**Challenges:** Political instability (Tigray conflict spillover), FX volatility (birr devaluation 2024), climate (erratic rains 2023/24).

**2026 focus:** Gender equity program (women-led co-ops), carbon footprint measurement, next-gen farmer retention.

Visit schedule: Nov–Jan harvest (lot selection), Jun–Aug (project review). Lena + Marcus + Ravi (content).
