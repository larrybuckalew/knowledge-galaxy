# Brand Design

Visual identity by **Studio Outline** (Portland, 2021). Refresh 2024.

**Logo system:** Primary wordmark (custom geometric sans), monogram "S" icon, badge variant for merchandise.

**Color palette:**
- Deep Space: #0B0D12 (primary background)
- Stellar Gold: #D4A843 (accent, [[Signature Series]])
- Nebula Purple: #6B4E8C (secondary, [[Subscription Program]] Connoisseur)
- Comet Blue: #3A7BC8 (tertiary, wholesale)
- Stardust: #E8E6E1 (text on dark)
- Void: #050608 (black space)

**Typography:** 
- Display: **Space Grotesk** (variable, 300–700)
- Body: **IBM Plex Sans** (regular, medium, semi-bold)
- Mono: **JetBrains Mono** (roast data, batch codes)

**Applications:** 
- Packaging: [[Packaging Team]] bags, [[Signature Series]] boxes, subscription inserts
- Cafe: Menu boards, signage, cups, merch
- Digital: Website, [[Email Marketing]], [[Instagram]] templates, [[Events]] materials
- Print: Wholesale catalogs, origin trip booklets, [[Farm Premium Fund]] reports

**Asset library:** Figma (team access), brand guidelines PDF (24 pages).
