# Quality Control

Every batch passes through three QC gates:

**Green Grading** (arrival)
- SCA green coffee classification: target Specialty Grade (0 primary defects, ≤5 secondary)
- Moisture: 10.0–12.0% (Sinowell meter)
- Water activity: <0.65 aw (Rotronic)
- 100g sample roasted on IKAWA Pro for [[Cupping Protocol]]

**Roast Verification** (post-roast, 24hr rest)
- ColorTrack reading: target Agtron 95 (light) / 75 (medium) / 55 (dark)
- Weight loss: 14–16% (light), 16–18% (medium), 18–20% (dark)
- Roast log vs profile: ΔBT <2°C, ΔET <3°C, time-to-first-crack ±15s

**Cupping Protocol** (SCA 0–100)
- Fragrance/aroma, flavor, aftertaste, acidity, body, balance, uniformity, clean cup, sweetness, overall
- Minimum 84 for release. 86+ = [[Signature Series]] designation.
- Panel: Marcus Chen, 2 baristas, 1 rotating guest. Blind, 5 cups/sample.

Failures trigger [[Roasting Process]] profile adjustment or [[Green Coffee Inventory]] hold.

Tools: Cropster, ColorTrack, IKAWA Pro, moisture meter, aw meter.
