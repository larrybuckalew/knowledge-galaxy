# Email Marketing

Platform: **Klaviyo** integrated with Shopify + ReCharge.

**Lists:** Subscribers (8.2K), Customers (4.1K), [[Subscription Program]] members (400+), [[Signature Series]] VIP (180).

**Campaigns:**
- Weekly "Roast Report" (Tue 9 AM) — new releases, roaster notes, brew tips. 42% open, 8% CTR.
- [[Signature Series]] launch sequence: Tease (T-14) → Reveal (T-7) → Launch (T-0) → Reminder (T+3) → Sold out (T+7).
- Abandoned cart (3-email): 1hr / 24hr / 72hr. 12% recovery.
- Post-purchase: Day 3 (brew guide), Day 14 (reorder), Day 30 (review request).
- Win-back: 60/90/120 day inactive. 6% reactivation.

**Automation flows:** Welcome series (5 emails), Browse abandonment, Price drop alert, Birthday (20% off).

**Segmentation:** Roast preference (light/medium/dark), Purchase frequency, AOV tier, Origin interest (from [[Cupping Protocol]] notes).

Design: [[Brand Design]] templates. Dark mode optimized. Plain-text fallback for deliverability.

Metrics dashboard: Weekly [[Team Structure]] review. Monthly benchmark vs coffee DTC peers.
