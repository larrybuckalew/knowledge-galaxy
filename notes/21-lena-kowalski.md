# Lena Kowalski — Founder/CEO

**Background:** 12 years specialty coffee. 2013–2018 Intelligentsia — green buyer (Ethiopia, Kenya, Colombia). 2018–2021 independent consultant (supply chain, roastery design). Founded Stellar 2021.

**Vision:** "Coffee as connection — farmer to cup, neighbor to neighbor." Direct trade not as marketing but as operational philosophy. Profitability enables impact.

**Responsibilities:**
- Company strategy & vision
- [[Supply Chain]] relationship ownership (annual origin trips)
- [[Financials]] oversight & fundraising
- [[Team Structure]] hiring/leadership
- [[Farm Premium Fund]] governance
- Board/advisor management

**Leadership style:** Radical transparency (open financials to team), delegation with accountability, monthly 1:1s with all direct reports. "Decide at the lowest competent level."

**Network:** SCA Board of Directors (2023–), World Coffee Research advisor, Portland Coffee Association founder. Mentors 3 early-stage coffee founders.

**Personal:** Competed US Brewers Cup 2016 (top 12). Home: Slayer Steam EP, Weber Workshops EG-1. Hikes Columbia Gorge weekly. Reads: economics, systems theory, poetry.
