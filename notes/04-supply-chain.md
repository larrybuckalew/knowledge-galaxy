# Supply Chain

Direct trade relationships with three primary cooperatives:

**Ethiopia — Yirgacheffe Farmers Cooperative Union (YFCU)**
- 300+ smallholder farmers, avg 2.5 hectares each
- Annual visit: harvest season (Nov–Jan) for lot selection
- Price premium: $1.80/lb above C-market, paid 50% upfront
- Contact: Alemayehu Bekele (export manager)

**Colombia — Cooperativa de Caficultores del Huila**
- 180 members, focus on Caturra & Pink Bourbon
- Visit: May–Jul (mitaca harvest) + Oct–Dec (main)
- Premium: $1.60/lb, 40% prepayment
- Contact: Maria Fernanda Gonzalez

**Guatemala — La Voz Que Clama en el Desierto**
- 45 families in Antigua valley, Bourbon/Typica
- Visit: Jan–Mar harvest
- Premium: $2.10/lb, 60% prepayment (higher risk)
- Contact: Carlos Mendez

Logistics: FOB origin → Matson/Ocean Network Express → Portland (28 days) → our warehouse. Customs broker: Pacific Rim Logistics.

Backup suppliers: [[Peru Cajamarca]], [[Costa Rica Tarrazu]] via Royal Coffee importer.
