# Marcus Chen — Head Roaster

**Background:** 8 years specialty coffee. 2017–2020 Coava Coffee (Portland) — production roaster. 2020–2021 Heart Coffee — assistant roaster. Joined Stellar 2021 as founding head roaster.

**Philosophy:** "Roast to reveal, not to impose." Light-touch profiles, convection heat for clarity. Opposed to "omni-roast" — believes each coffee deserves unique curve.

**Responsibilities:**
- [[Roasting Process]] development & execution
- [[Quality Control]] final sign-off
- [[Cupping Protocol]] panel lead
- [[Green Coffee Inventory]] purchase decisions (with Lena)
- [[Signature Series]] selection
- Equipment maintenance scheduling ([[Equipment]])
- [[Barista Team]] espresso dial-in oversight

**Certifications:** Q-Grader (2022, renewed 2024), SCA Roasting Professional, SCA Sensory Professional.

**Notable profiles:** Ethiopia Yirgacheffe "Starlight" (Agtron 98, 9:45 FC), Colombia Pink Bourbon "Aurora" (honey, extended Maillard), Guatemala "Horizon" (washed, 10:30 drop).

**Personal:** Home setup — IKAWA Pro, Comandante C40, Fellow Stagg EKG. Collects vintage coffee tins. Speaks Mandarin (family), learning Spanish for origin trips.
