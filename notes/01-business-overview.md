# Business Overview

**Stellar Coffee Roasters** is a specialty coffee company founded in 2021, located in the heart of Portland's Pearl District. We source premium beans directly from farmers in Ethiopia, Colombia, and Guatemala, focusing on sustainable practices and fair trade relationships. Our mission is to bring exceptional coffee experiences to our community while supporting the farmers who make it possible.

We operate a 2,400 sq ft roastery and cafe space that serves as both production facility and community hub. Our team of 12 includes head roaster Marcus Chen, three baristas, two packaging specialists, and a small admin/operations team.

Key differentiators: direct trade relationships (visiting origin farms annually), precision roasting on a Loring S35 Peregrine, and a subscription program serving 400+ monthly members.
