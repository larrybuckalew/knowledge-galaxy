# Farm Premium Fund

Established 2023. 40% of [[Signature Series]] revenue + $0.50/lb from [[Subscription Program]] Connoisseur/Collector tiers.

**2025 allocations ($72K total):**
- YFCU (Ethiopia): $28K — washing station upgrade (eco-pulper, raised beds)
- Huila Co-op (Colombia): $22K — solar drying canopy, cupping lab equipment
- La Voz (Guatemala): $15K — seedling nursery, shade tree program
- Emergency reserve: $7K (climate events, crop failure)

**Governance:** Lena Kowalski + Marcus Chen + cooperative reps (annual video call). Transparency report published Q1 following year.

**Impact tracking:** Yield per hectare, cup score trend, farmer income survey (third-party).

**2026 targets:** $100K fund. New partner: Peru Cajamarca co-op (experimental anaerobic lots).
