# Costa Rica Tarrazu

**Farm:** Finca El Diamante (family-owned, 12 hectares, 1,650m)
**Variety:** Catuai Rojo, Villa Sarchi
**Process:** Honey (red, 50% mucilage), Washed
**Harvest:** November–March

**Current lot:** 6 bags arrived Nov 2025 (new crop). Cupping: 85.75 — bright, red fruit, honey sweetness, clean finish. [[Quality Control]] approved.

**Relationship:** Direct since 2023. Annual visit (Jan harvest). Premium: $1.70/lb, 50% prepayment. Contact: Diego Herrera (owner, 3rd generation).

**Sustainability:** Carbon neutral certified (2022). Solar drying, water recycling, biodiversity corridors. Bird-friendly habitat (Golden-winged Warbler).

**Roasting notes:** Honey loves heat — charge 200°C, extended Maillard to 8:30, drop 10:15 (medium). Washed: standard profile.

**Allocation:** 60% [[Subscription Program]] (Connoisseur/Collector), 25% cafe, 15% wholesale.
