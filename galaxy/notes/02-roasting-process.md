# Roasting Process

Our roasting philosophy centers on highlighting each bean's unique terroir. We use a **Loring S35 Peregrine** — a 35kg convection roaster that gives us precise temperature control and repeatable profiles.

**Standard profile phases:**
1. **Drying** (0:00–4:30) — Charge temp 190°C, fan at 70%, drum 55 RPM. Goal: even moisture removal without scorching.
2. **Maillard** (4:30–8:00) — Ramp to 205°C, fan 65%. Development of sugars and amino acids.
3. **Development** (8:00–11:30) — First crack at ~9:45. Drop at 10:30 for light, 11:00 for medium, 11:30 for dark.

We cup every batch 24 hours post-roast using SCA protocol. Roast logs stored in [[Cropster]] with batch IDs linked to [[Green Coffee Inventory]].

Marcus Chen (head roaster) adjusts profiles per origin — Ethiopians get faster development, Colombians more Maillard time.
